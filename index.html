    How to play
    • Press Ready, then roll the die. That many days pass and every price moves. News can jolt a single stock.
    • Every stock has a hidden personality, calm or wild, trending up or down. It is new every game, so read the charts.
    • The percent next to each price is measured from your average buy price for that stock, or from the starting price if you do not own it.
    • After the last roll you get a final chance to sell. Shares you still hold are worth nothing, only cash counts.
